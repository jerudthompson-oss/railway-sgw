# Red Barn Commerce — Iowa Farm Prospect List

**Built:** 2026-07-29
**Origin point:** Gilbert, IA (Story County)
**Target radius:** ~45–75 min drive — far enough to be strangers, close enough to visit a market
**Purpose:** feed the 20-farm cold email send (Week 2 of the launch plan)

---

## Read this before using the list

**This list is candidate names, not verified contacts.** Every farm below came from
live web search and is a real business. But two things are *not* verified and you
must confirm them yourself before sending:

1. **Email addresses.** I could not retrieve any. Sending to a guessed address
   (`info@`, `contact@`) burns your brand-new domain's sending reputation on
   bounces, which is genuinely costly in week one. Get the real address off the
   farm's own site or Facebook "About" tab.
2. **Whether they already have online ordering.** This is the actual
   qualification. A farm that already runs a store is not a prospect.

**A structural problem worth naming:** web search surfaces farms *that have
websites*. Your ideal customer — the farm running Thanksgiving orders out of
Facebook comments — is close to invisible to search precisely because they have
no site. So this list skews toward farms that are further along than your ideal
prospect. The three sources that actually contain Facebook-only farms are listed
in §4, and all three require you to be logged in or physically present. That's
30 minutes of your own clicking that I can't do from here.

## 4. How to finish the list to 20 — ~30 min of your own clicking

I got blocked at every directory (this environment's network policy 403s all page
fetches; only search worked). These three sources hold the Facebook-only farms
that search can't see:

1. **Ames Main Street Farmers' Market 2026 vendor page** —
   `downtownames.org/farmersmarket/vendor/`. 95+ vendors, and the market emails
   the roster: `amesfarmersmarket@amesalliance.com`. Vendors here farm all over
   central Iowa, so it is *not* just a Story County list. This is your single
   densest source.
2. **Choose Iowa profiles directory** — `chooseiowa.com/profiles-directory`.
   State ag department program, filterable, and members have opted into being
   found by buyers. Warmer than a scraped list.
3. **Iowa Beef Industry Council local beef directory** —
   `iabeef.org/raising-beef/iowa-local-beef-directory`. Browsable **by county**,
   and every farm on it sells direct to consumers by definition. For the
   Thanksgiving-bundle pitch this is the highest-intent list of the three.

Also worth 10 minutes: **Facebook search** for "farm" restricted to Marshalltown,
Newton, Webster City, Iowa Falls, Jefferson. A farm with an active Facebook page
and no website link is your exact customer, and this is the only way to find them.

### Qualification test — 60 seconds per farm

Keep a farm only if **all four** are true:

- [ ] Sells **direct to consumers** (CSA, bundles, market stand) — not wholesale/commodity
- [ ] **No working online ordering.** A Facebook page, a Linktree, or a brochure site with a phone number all count as *no store* — those are qualified, not disqualified
- [ ] Has a **fall or holiday product**: CSA signups, Thanksgiving turkeys, meat bundles, pumpkins, cider
- [ ] You have a **real email address**, found on their own page

Drop anyone you know socially. Drop anyone with a Fareway connection.
