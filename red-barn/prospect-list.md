## Why Choose Iowa is the right source

It's the Iowa Department of Agriculture's member program. Two properties matter:

- **Members opted in to being found by buyers.** That's a warmer starting posture than a scraped list, and it means an unsolicited email about selling more is on-topic rather than out of nowhere.
- **Profiles carry street addresses**, so distance is verifiable instead of guessed.

**Rev 2 corrected two errors from my first pass**, both from Choose Iowa profiles:
- **Dropseed Farm is near Dubuque**, not the Des Moines metro. It's ~3 hours out. Removed.
- **Long Walk Farm is in Council Bluffs**, not central Iowa. Removed.

Still true, and still the main limit: **I have no email addresses.** The profile
pages carry them but this environment 403s every page fetch, so the addresses are
one click away for you and unreachable for me. Guessing `info@` would burn your
new domain's sending reputation on bounces in exactly the week that matters.

## 4. Your 30 minutes — get the emails

Everything below needs a browser I don't have.

1. **Choose Iowa profiles directory** — `chooseiowa.com/profiles-directory`.
   Filterable, and it takes a `?category=` URL parameter (e.g. `?category=342`).
   Filter to your counties — **Hardin, Marshall, Jasper, Greene, Boone, Poweshiek,
   Hamilton, Grundy** — and pull the email off each profile. This alone should
   close Tier B and take you past 20.
2. **ISU Extension "Visit Iowa Farms"** — `visitiowafarms.org/farm-search`.
   Second searchable directory, different membership. Good for farms Choose Iowa
   missed.
3. **Iowa Beef Industry Council directory** —
   `iabeef.org/raising-beef/iowa-local-beef-directory`. Browsable **by county**,
   and every listing sells direct by definition. Highest-intent list for the
   Thanksgiving-bundle pitch.
4. **Ames Main Street Farmers' Market** — `downtownames.org/farmersmarket/vendor/`,
   95+ vendors who farm all over central Iowa, not just Story County. The market
   will email the roster: `amesfarmersmarket@amesalliance.com`. This is how you
   place Fisher's Flowers and Funcke Fresh Farm.
5. **Facebook search** for "farm" in Marshalltown, Newton, Webster City, Iowa
   Falls, Jefferson, Grundy Center. An active page with no website link is your
   exact customer — and this is the only way to find them, because search engines
   only surface farms that already have sites.

**One non-obvious channel:** **West Forty Market** (stores in Ankeny and Greene)
sources from **7 small Iowa farms**. It's disqualified as a customer but it's a
list of seven qualified suppliers, and a warm intro beats a cold email.

### Qualification test — 60 seconds per farm

Keep only if **all four** are true:

- [ ] Sells **direct to consumers** — not wholesale or commodity
- [ ] **No working online ordering.** A Facebook page, a Linktree, or a brochure site with a phone number all count as *no store* — those qualify, they don't disqualify
- [ ] Has a **fall or holiday product**: CSA signups, turkeys, meat bundles, pumpkins, cider, holiday greens
- [ ] You have a **real email address** off their own page
- [ ] **They don't sell into your employer** — the Van Manen catch. Check this every time

Drop anyone you know socially.

## 5. Honest count

- **4** Tier A — researched, qualified, emails drafted
- **12** Tier B — real names, needing one field
- **5** held back for the stranger-first rule
- **22** disqualified for distance, existing stores, wrong model, or employer conflict

**The number that matters: deep research killed 4 of 8 Tier A farms.** That's a
~50% kill rate, which means **you need to qualify roughly 40 farms to send 20.**
Budget accordingly — and note that a farm only looks qualified until you actually
read about it.

**Don't let this list become the project.** Failure mode #1 is spending both
weeks on assets and sending zero emails. If §4 turns into a research afternoon,
stop and send to whoever passed — twelve real farms beats twenty perfect rows.
